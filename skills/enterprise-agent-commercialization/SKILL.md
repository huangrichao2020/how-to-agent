# Enterprise Agent Commercialization

Turn enterprise agent work into a sellable business-change offer.

## Core Stance

Do not sell "an AI agent" first. Sell a clearer diagnosis of the buyer's own
business process, then turn that diagnosis into a visible artifact and a paid
next step.

```text
business pain -> process map -> priority scenarios -> skill specs
  -> workshop proof -> quick win -> rollout -> renewal
```

## When To Use

Use this skill when the user asks for:

- enterprise agent product design;
- AI training or CEO workshop design;
- agent consulting, diagnostics, or implementation offers;
- sales conversion, follow-up, retention, or renewal;
- turning business scenarios into paid skill packages.

## Output Standards

A good answer produces business artifacts, not generic advice:

- buyer profile and trigger event;
- process pain map;
- top 3-5 agent scenarios;
- A/B/C skill type portfolio;
- workshop or diagnostic offer;
- conversion hooks;
- 30/60/90 day rollout;
- renewal or advisory offer;
- risks and human-review boundaries.

## Workflow

1. **Name the buyer and trigger.**
   Identify who pays, what event made the problem urgent, and what business
   metric they care about.

2. **Map the management layer.**
   List core and support workflows. Score each by repeat frequency, time cost,
   strategic value, and rule clarity. Pick the top 3-5 AI transformation
   scenarios.

3. **Decompose the execution layer.**
   Break each priority scenario into atomic actions with input, output, data
   shape, error risk, and human-review need.

4. **Classify skill types.**
   Use:
   - A: full automation for rule-heavy, low-risk tasks;
   - B: AI-led workflow with human confirmation;
   - C: AI-assisted decision where people keep ownership.

5. **Design the paid entry product.**
   Prefer a diagnostic workshop that produces a maturity map, process map,
   scenario priority matrix, first skill spec, and rollout proposal.

6. **Build the conversion path.**
   Define hooks for acquisition, consultation, delivery, and renewal. Each hook
   should create a reason for the next conversation without fake urgency.

7. **Package the rollout.**
   Write a 30/60/90 day plan:
   - 0-30: quick wins and type A tasks;
   - 30-60: AI-led workflows and team adoption;
   - 60-90: decision support, dashboards, and governance.

8. **Make proof visible.**
   Plan before/after reports, adoption metrics, saved time, quality changes,
   and authorized case snippets for renewal and referral.

## Conversion Hooks

| Stage | Hook |
| --- | --- |
| Acquisition | A counterintuitive industry pain or benchmark |
| Consultation | A diagnostic question the buyer cannot answer alone |
| Delivery | A concrete artifact produced from the buyer's own workflow |
| Renewal | A before/after proof plus the next leverage point |

## Anti-Patterns

- Starting with model choice, vendor choice, or prompt tips.
- Selling custom development before a paid diagnosis.
- Teaching executives tool operations instead of business leverage.
- Giving away the whole plan during the first consultation.
- Using fake scarcity or discounts instead of opportunity cost.
- Automating high-risk decisions without review boundaries.
- Calling a generic training deck an enterprise agent strategy.
