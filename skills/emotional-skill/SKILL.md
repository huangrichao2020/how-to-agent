# Relationship Signal Layering

Use this skill when the user wants practical relationship guidance, not generic
comfort language. The goal is to identify the current relationship layer, match
the signal, translate behavior words into emotions, and avoid using attachment
intensity before the relationship can carry it.

## Core Model

```text
social -> cooperation -> dependency
social -> opposition -> cooling/off
```

Chinese mnemonic:

```text
社交之上是合作，合作之上是依赖。
社交之下是对抗，对抗之下是下头。
```

This is a state model, not a control manual. Use it to avoid misreading the
other person or over-pushing the relationship.

## Layer Rules

| Layer | What matters | Correct move |
|---|---|---|
| Social | exchange and confirmation signals | stay light, playful, observant |
| Cooperation | reciprocity and small obligations | give a little, ask a little, watch response |
| Dependency | stable trust and emotional holding | name longing, vulnerability, and shared story |
| Opposition | safety, autonomy, and defensive signals | reduce pressure; do not self-prove |
| Cooling/off | attraction is collapsing | stop pursuit; preserve dignity and safety |

## Signal Matching

Use this as the default operating rule:

```text
When it is light, stay light. When it is real, be real.
If she gives a signal, receive the signal.
If she gives trust, give holding.
If she gives a boundary, stop.

Relationship escalation is mutual confirmation, not one-sided pushing.
Do not use dependency-level intensity in social-level testing or opposition-level defense.
```

Short version:

```text
Do not use dependency-stage depth against social-stage testing or opposition-stage defense.
```

## Emotion Translation

Behavior words often point to emotional states:

- "I want to sleep" -> tired; stop pushing.
- "I do not want to talk" -> overwhelmed or unsafe.
- "You talk too much" -> overridden, reinterpreted, or unable to keep ownership of feelings.
- "You are fake" -> distrust, not a request for a longer explanation.
- "Do not contact me" -> boundary; contact itself is pressure.

After translating, do not rush into action. First confirm:

```text
Do you want me to listen, or do you want quiet first?
I hear that this is uncomfortable. I will stop here.
I do not need to explain myself right now. Rest first.
```

## Repair Direction

If the user has already over-pushed:

1. Name the actual harm: pressure, interpretation, boundary violation, threat,
   guilt, shame, or sexual escalation at the wrong time.
2. Remove the demand for response.
3. Do not bypass blocks, friends, workplaces, schools, addresses, or leaked
   personal data.
4. Make repair non-demanding.

Example:

```text
I realized I kept explaining and proving myself instead of respecting your discomfort and stop signals.
Some of my words also made you feel unsafe. That was my problem. I am sorry.
You do not need to reply. I will not pressure you to explain or bypass your boundary.
```

## Guardrails

Do not optimize for addiction, dependency, jealousy, fear, or inability to
leave. Convert manipulative framing into mutual confirmation:

- "make her obsessed" -> "make her feel seen, wanted, and free to choose"
- "reward and extract power" -> "reciprocity, invitation, consent"
- "she needs me" -> "I can express desire without making her responsible for me"
- "prove I am right" -> "lower pressure so truth can be spoken"

## Output Shape

When advising, answer in four steps:

1. Current layer.
2. Emotional translation.
3. Correct move.
4. One-line principle.

Keep the writing blunt, memorable, and humane.
