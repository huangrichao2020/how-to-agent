# Longform Writing Quality

Borrow useful writing QA ideas from `KKKKhazix/khazix-skills` without making any personal author voice the default. Mimic a named style only when the user explicitly asks.

## HKR Before Writing

- Happy: is there curiosity, surprise, or a reason to keep reading?
- Knowledge: will the reader learn something concrete?
- Resonance: does it touch a real anxiety, desire, delight, or lived situation?

If only one dimension is present, propose a sharper angle before drafting. If material is thin, ask for missing facts, personal experience, examples, or emotional position.

## Four-Layer Review

1. Hard scan: remove generic AI openings, boilerplate endings, unsupported claims, and excessive structure.
2. Rhythm: vary paragraph length and keep transitions natural.
3. Substance: every major point needs an example, source, or lived observation.
4. Human read: rewrite cold paragraphs until they sound like a person with a view.

## Output

When drafting, provide the article unless process is requested. When reviewing, lead with the highest-impact edits.
