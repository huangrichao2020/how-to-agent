# Relationship Signal Layering

Some relationship advice sounds correct but unusable: "respect boundaries",
"communicate well", "be sincere". The missing part is layer awareness. A move
that feels warm in one layer can feel invasive in another.

## Core Model

```text
social -> cooperation -> dependency
social -> opposition -> cooling/off
```

Chinese mnemonic:

```text
社交之上是合作，合作之上是依赖。
社交之下是对抗，对抗之下是下头。
```

The practical insight is simple:

```text
Do not use dependency-stage depth against social-stage testing or opposition-stage defense.
```

## Layers

Social is signal exchange. The other person may joke, test, flirt, pull back, or
say something ambiguous. The right move is not confession or pressure. The right
move is signal matching.

Cooperation starts when both sides invest a little. One person gives attention,
the other gives response. One asks for a small favor, the other accepts or
negotiates. Relationship energy becomes reciprocal.

Dependency is the layer where deeper words can finally land. Longing,
vulnerability, shared narrative, and "I need you" become warm only after trust
can carry them.

Opposition is not a puzzle to solve by talking harder. It often means the other
person is defending autonomy, safety, or emotional ownership. The correct move
is to lower pressure.

Cooling/off means attraction is collapsing. More pursuit usually accelerates
the collapse. Preserve dignity, stop pressure, and repair only if a real channel
opens.

## Behavior Words Are Emotion Words

People often say a behavior while pointing to an emotion:

```text
I want to sleep        -> I am tired; stop pushing.
I do not want to talk  -> I am overwhelmed or unsafe.
You talk too much      -> I feel overridden.
You are fake           -> I do not trust this performance.
Do not contact me      -> Contact itself is pressure.
```

After translating the emotion, do not rush into another move. Confirm the state:

```text
Do you want me to listen, or do you want quiet first?
I hear that this is uncomfortable. I will stop here.
I do not need to explain myself right now. Rest first.
```

## Signal Matching

Memorize this:

```text
When it is light, stay light. When it is real, be real.
If she gives a signal, receive the signal.
If she gives trust, give holding.
If she gives a boundary, stop.

Relationship escalation is mutual confirmation, not one-sided pushing.
Do not use dependency-stage intensity before the relationship can carry it.
```

## Non-Manipulation Boundary

This method is not for making someone addicted, jealous, afraid, or unable to
leave. It is for reading the layer correctly enough that desire does not turn
into pressure.

Translate manipulative goals into mutual confirmation:

```text
make her obsessed -> let her feel seen, wanted, and free to choose
extract power     -> reciprocity, invitation, and consent
prove I am right  -> lower pressure so truth can be spoken
```

The strongest form of desire is not endless pursuit. It is the ability to move
forward when invited and stop when stopped.
